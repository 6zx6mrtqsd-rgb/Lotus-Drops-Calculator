Enter the recipe's Mg / Ca / Na / K values directly. Drops = ppm × litres ÷ (mg/L added per drop per litre). The drop-strength defaults were back-solved from a single Lotus calculator result (round dropper, 900 mL), so they are estimates (whole-drop rounding leaves roughly ±10–40% uncertainty, most for Na and K). Refine them by running a large volume on the Lotus page. Starting TDS is treated as ~50% GH / 50% KH and subtracted from the target GH and KH, keeping the Ca/Mg and K/Na ratios. Leave it at 0 for distilled or RO water.
